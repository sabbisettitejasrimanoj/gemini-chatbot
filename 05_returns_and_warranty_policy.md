# Returns, Exchanges & Warranty Policy

## Return Window

Unworn footwear may be returned within 45 days of the delivery date for a full refund to the original payment method. Items must be returned in their original packaging with all tags attached. Shoes that show signs of outdoor wear, such as scuffed soles or dirt, are not eligible for return.

## Exchange Process

Customers may exchange for a different size or color within the same 45-day window at no additional shipping cost. To initiate an exchange, use the online returns portal to generate a prepaid shipping label, then package the original pair for return. The replacement pair ships once the original is scanned by the carrier.

## Final Sale Items

Items marked "Final Sale," including clearance footwear discounted more than 40% off the original price, are not eligible for return or exchange except in cases of manufacturing defect.

## Manufacturing Defects

Footwear found to have a manufacturing defect — such as sole separation, stitching failure, or material defects occurring within 12 months of purchase under normal wear conditions — is covered under warranty for a free repair, replacement, or store credit, at the company's discretion. Damage resulting from improper care, accidents, or normal wear and tear (such as worn-down outsoles from extended use) is not covered.

## How to File a Warranty Claim

1. Contact customer support with your order number and a description of the issue.
2. Provide clear photos showing the defect.
3. A support representative will review the claim within 3 to 5 business days.
4. If approved, a prepaid return label will be issued, and a replacement or refund will be processed once the item is received and inspected.

## International Returns

International orders may be returned following the same 45-day policy, but the customer is responsible for return shipping costs unless the item is defective. Customs duties and taxes paid at the time of import are non-refundable.

## Refund Processing Time

Refunds are processed within 5 to 10 business days after the returned item is received at the warehouse. The time for the refund to appear on a statement depends on the customer's bank or card issuer, typically an additional 3 to 7 business days.
