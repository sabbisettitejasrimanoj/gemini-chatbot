# Footwear Product Catalog

## Running Shoes

### AeroStride Runner
The AeroStride Runner is a lightweight neutral running shoe designed for daily training. It features a breathable engineered mesh upper, a dual-density foam midsole for balanced cushioning and responsiveness, and a durable carbon rubber outsole for traction on both road and light trail surfaces. Weight: 265g (men's size 9). Available in 6 colorways. Price: $129.99.

### TrailBlaze GTX
Built for off-road conditions, the TrailBlaze GTX combines a waterproof Gore-Tex membrane with an aggressive lugged outsole for grip on mud, gravel, and wet rock. A rock plate in the forefoot protects against sharp debris. Recommended for trail runs up to marathon distance. Price: $159.99.

### Velocity Racer
A minimalist racing flat built for speed on race day. Features a plate-embedded nylon midsole for propulsion, a snug knit upper, and minimal heel-to-toe drop (4mm) for a natural running motion. Not recommended as a daily trainer due to reduced cushioning. Price: $199.99.
